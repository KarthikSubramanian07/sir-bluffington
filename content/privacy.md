# Privacy policy

> Sir Bluffington's Poker does not collect, sell, or share personal data. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, and no advertising. Your settings and stats stay in your own browser.

Last updated: 8 October 2026.

## What is stored, and where

The game saves two things in your browser's local storage so they survive a page reload: your table settings (table size, opponents, stack, blinds, blind structure, and deck style) and your play statistics (hands played, showdown results, biggest pot, net chips, and your record against each bot). This data never leaves your device. The game has no server to send it to. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

## What is not collected

There are no accounts, sign-ups, or email lists. The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, tracking pixels, advertising, or third-party scripts. Fonts and all other assets are served from the site itself, so loading the page does not contact any other company. No real money changes hands, so there is no payment information.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical request data (such as IP addresses and browser user agents) to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. That processing is governed by [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). This project does not receive or keep those logs for any tracking purpose.

## Children

The game uses play money only and is not gambling, but it is a poker game and is intended for a general adult audience. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it, and the history is public in the project's GitHub repository. Questions about privacy are welcome through the [contact page](https://sirbluffington.pages.dev/contact).
